## 3. Most Relevant arXiv Papers (theme: on-device / agentic AI on smartphones)

1. **SchemaWeaver: Accelerating On-Device Mobile Agentic AI via Schema-Guided Parallel LLM Decoding** — Boyuan Yang, Wei Gao (Oct 2026). Tackles the core bottleneck of on-device mobile AI agents: long LLM tool-calling latency, via schema-guided parallel decoding. **arXiv:2610.17436**
2. **PalmClaw: A Native On-Device Agent Framework for Mobile Phones** — Cai, Li, Wei, Li (Jul 2026). Moves LLM agents (tool use, multi-step execution) natively onto phones rather than desktops/servers. **arXiv:2607.13027**
3. **AgBench: Agentic AI Benchmarks for Personal AI Devices** — Han, Wu, Saikumar, Varghese (Sep 2026). Benchmarks agentic AI executing locally on personal devices — the cost/privacy tradeoff that motivates on-device agents. **arXiv:2609.38652**

*Also useful: MNN-LLM: A Generic Inference Engine for Fast LLM Deployment on Mobile Devices — arXiv:2506.10443; "The Battery Price of Edge AI" (environmental impact of on-device LLM inference) — arXiv:2609.11940.*
